Health and Well-being Track

Healthy Mind. Healthy Body. Confident Comeback.

01 THE FOUNDATION OF A COMEBACK

Make space for well-being.

The Health & Well-being Track at the IEEE Returning Mothers Conference 2026 is designed to help returning mothers, professionals, and caregivers prioritize their physical, mental, and emotional well-being while navigating career transitions and personal responsibilities.

Aligned with the conference theme, “The Future is Shared: Changing the Story of Work, Care, and Confidence,” this track highlights the importance of well-being as a foundation for career success, resilience, and personal fulfillment.
