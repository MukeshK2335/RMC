IEEE RMC 2026 · Pre-Conference Webinar Series · Season 1, Session 1

From Idea to Startup: Building the Foundation of Your Business

Understanding Customers and Building Your Brand

27 June 2026 Featuring Dr. Prashant Joshi

About the webinar

Introduction

The IEEE Returning Mothers Conference (RMC) 2026 Outreach Committee successfully organized the first session of the Pre-Conference Webinar Series – Season 1 as part of its initiatives leading to the IEEE Returning Mothers Conference 2026. The webinar was designed to empower professionals, students, entrepreneurs, and individuals returning to their careers after a break by providing practical knowledge, inspiration, and opportunities for professional growth.

The session served as a platform for participants to gain insights into entrepreneurship, innovation, customer-centric product development, startup strategy, and brand building. It also introduced participants to the vision and objectives of the IEEE Returning Mothers Conference 2026 and highlighted the role of the Outreach Committee in fostering collaboration, networking, and lifelong learning within the IEEE community.

The webinar brought together IEEE volunteers, student members, faculty members, researchers, entrepreneurs, industry professionals, and aspiring innovators from different institutions.

Learning goals

Objectives

  • Introduce the IEEE Returning Mothers Conference 2026 and its vision of empowering individuals returning to professional careers after career breaks.
  • Familiarize participants with the initiatives and activities of the IEEE RMC Outreach Committee.
  • Provide practical knowledge on entrepreneurship, startup development, and innovation.
  • Help participants understand customer discovery, value creation, and market validation.
  • Build awareness of customer-centric product development and business sustainability.
  • Motivate aspiring entrepreneurs to transform innovative ideas into scalable businesses.
  • Encourage networking, collaboration, leadership development, and knowledge sharing among IEEE members and professionals.
  • Prepare participants for the upcoming IEEE Returning Mothers Conference 2026.

The session

Event Proceedings

The webinar commenced with a warm welcome address by Ms. Thanishka Yogesh, who welcomed the distinguished speaker, organizing committee members, volunteers, IEEE members, and participants. She introduced the objectives of the Pre-Conference Webinar Series and its importance in supporting professionals and returning mothers through knowledge sharing, networking, and community engagement.

Ms. Sivasankari G.S. introduced the IEEE Returning Mothers Conference 2026 and explained its vision of empowering individuals returning to the workforce after career breaks through continuous learning, skill enhancement, and professional development. She acknowledged the vision of Prof. Ramalatha Marimuthu, Founding Chair of the conference, and shared details of the upcoming conference scheduled for 18–19 September 2026 at Hotel Kalyan, Greater Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

Mr. Moreshwar Naikwadi introduced the IEEE RMC Outreach Committee and described its mission to connect IEEE members, student branches, educational institutions, volunteers, and industry professionals through outreach initiatives, leadership development programmes, community engagement, workshops, and networking opportunities.

Ms. Smrithi introduced the keynote speaker, Dr. Prashant Joshi, highlighting his experience as a Systems Engineer, Systems Architect, Venture Builder, and Founder and CEO of NeepenScale. She noted his work designing future-ready technological systems and mentoring entrepreneurs in transforming innovative ideas into scalable businesses.

Dr. Joshi began by appreciating the vision of the Returning Mothers Conference and sharing personal experiences connected to its theme. He encouraged participants to value hard work, smart work, perseverance, and continuous learning as foundations for entrepreneurial success.

He presented four fundamental pillars of entrepreneurship—Idea, Customer, Value, and Brand—and emphasized that entrepreneurship is about solving meaningful customer problems and creating sustainable value. Revenue, he explained, follows from consistently delivering value. He distinguished an idea, a product, and a sustainable business, highlighting customer trust and repeat purchases, and introduced desirability, feasibility, and viability as foundations for sustainable startups.

The session introduced the Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD) framework, helping participants understand customers through the situations they experience rather than demographic categories. Through case studies of the Iridium satellite communication system, CAN-based vehicle diagnostics, and the Sensoto SR100 platform, Dr. Joshi illustrated how market timing, customer readiness, technological evolution, and perceived value influence product success.

Dr. Joshi discussed systems thinking, customer interaction, product validation, and the importance of identifying measurable customer outcomes before launching products. He emphasized translating engineering features into meaningful customer benefits rather than focusing only on technical specifications.

An interactive question-and-answer session followed, with participants asking about entrepreneurship, startup challenges, customer understanding, business sustainability, and brand building. An E-Memento was presented to Dr. Joshi in recognition of his contribution.

Participants were invited to complete an online feedback form. The programme concluded with an official group photograph and a Vote of Thanks delivered by Dr. Thanuja, who expressed gratitude to the keynote speaker, organizing committee, volunteers, participants, and everyone who contributed to the webinar.

Community

What participants gained

Outcome

The webinar enhanced participants’ understanding of entrepreneurship, startup ecosystems, customer-centric innovation, product development, and strategic brand building. Participants gained practical knowledge on transforming ideas into sustainable business models by understanding customer needs, validating market opportunities, and creating long-term customer value.

The session also strengthened awareness of the IEEE Returning Mothers Conference 2026 and encouraged participants to engage in future pre-conference activities. It inspired attendees to adopt an innovation-driven mindset while promoting professional development, collaboration, and lifelong learning.

Looking ahead

Conclusion

The inaugural session of the IEEE Returning Mothers Conference 2026 Pre-Conference Webinar Series concluded successfully with enthusiastic participation and engagement. Dr. Prashant Joshi’s keynote offered valuable insights into entrepreneurship, innovation, customer discovery, product development, and brand building through practical examples and real-world experiences.

The webinar achieved its objectives by providing actionable knowledge, encouraging entrepreneurial thinking, and strengthening connections within the IEEE community. Its success marks a strong beginning to the Pre-Conference Webinar Series and reinforces the commitment of the IEEE Returning Mothers Conference 2026 to empowering professionals through continuous learning, collaboration, and innovative thinking.
