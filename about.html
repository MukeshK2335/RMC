01 OUR PURPOSE

What is Returning Mothers Conference?

Returning Mothers Conference supports people on a career break who are preparing to re-enter the workforce. We believe the way forward begins with the opportunity to build skills, reconnect with confidence, and take the next step at your own pace.

Through learning, mentoring, and meaningful connections, RMC brings together what is needed and what is possible—helping create sustainable careers across academia, industry, and entrepreneurship.

02 THE COMMUNITY

Who should attend RMC?

  • Graduate and undergraduate students, and professionals at any career stage
  • Anyone planning a career break, currently on a break, or preparing to return to work
  • Senior professionals who want to mentor the next generation and be inspired in return
  • People looking for collaboration, networking, life skills, and motivation
  • Those who want to give back to the community
  • Aspiring entrepreneurs looking for mentorship to grow an idea into a startup
03 YOUR NEXT CHAPTER

What will attendees gain?

  • Discover your strengths and turn them into new opportunities
  • Build interpersonal and intrapersonal skills for leadership and entrepreneurship
  • Strengthen public speaking and technical communication skills
  • Grow your network through collaboration and meaningful connections
  • Find mentors and fresh perspectives to help shape your next steps
  • Feel empowered to create a supportive environment at home, work, and in society
