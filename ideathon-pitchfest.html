The Ideathon and Pitchfest track is a dynamic platform designed to empower women re-entering the workforce by transforming their ideas into actionable ventures. It blends creativity, innovation, and entrepreneurial thinking, offering participants an opportunity to rediscover their professional identity while addressing real-world challenges. This track serves as a bridge between potential and opportunity, providing a supportive environment that fosters collaboration and equips returning mothers with the tools needed to enter the professional world as innovators and founders.

Your idea, made actionable

How to submit your abstract step by step

Seven clear steps take your idea from first details to a finished proposal.

  1. Open the portal IEEE RMC 2026 Ideathon & Pitchfest and fully complete the team registration fields.
  2. In the next section, provide your Problem Statement, Proposed Solution, and Impact Assessment.
  3. Outline your Primary Objective, Innovation/Differentiation, and Current Stage of Development.
  4. Define your Scope of Implementation, Resource Requirements, and Scalability Plan.
  5. Detail your Awareness of Support Mechanisms, Feasibility/Confidence Levels, and Mentorship Requirements.
  6. Share your Inspiration Behind the Idea and its Relevance to Returning Mothers.
  7. Agree to the declaration and click Submit to finalize your proposal.

What Happens Next?

You’ll receive email updates at key stages:

  1. Submission Received

  2. Under Review

  3. Accepted

Abstract submission deadline 10 JUL 2026
  1. 01

    Phase 01

    Abstract Submission

    Submit your idea using the official abstract template available on the portal. All entries will be evaluated based on originality, feasibility, and potential impact. Shortlisted teams will be announced following the review process.

  2. 02

    Phase 02

    Mentorship & Workshops

    Shortlisted teams will be paired with mentors for guidance and feedback.

    • Workshop 1: Open to all participants, focused on idea validation and presentation skills.
    • Workshop 2 / Panel: Exclusive to shortlisted teams, with expert insights to prepare for the finals.
  3. 03

    Phase 03

    Final Pitchfest

    Finalists present their ideas to a panel of investors, leaders, and experts. Winning teams are selected based on innovation, scalability, and social impact.

  4. 04

    Phase 04

    Results & Recognition

    Winners, runners-up, and women-led teams are recognized. Certificates and prize money are awarded to participants.
